01 — Method

Four moves, every single unit

Always in this order. It takes about twelve minutes and it is the whole reason the activities will feel easy later.

  1. 01

    Read the rule

    2 minutes

    Read only two boxes: Your mouth and The one difference. Ignore everything else on the card for now.

  2. 02

    Find the place

    2 minutes

    Look at the cross-section. The pink point is where your sound is made. Say the sound and feel that exact spot with your tongue or lips.

  3. 03

    Listen & repeat

    5 minutes

    Play each word twice. Then the pairs: listen → repeat out loud → listen again. Never repeat silently in your head.

  4. 04

    Check yourself

    3 minutes

    Mirror for the lips and tongue. Hand on your throat for voicing. If it vibrates, the sound is voiced.

The rules of this stage

  • Nothing here is graded, and no score is saved.
  • You may look at the words as many times as you want.
  • In the activities there is no text — only sound. That is where you identify it alone.
  • Two units per session. Five sessions. Do not do ten in one night.

Unit 01 · /s/ /z/

Front of the tongue close to the tooth ridge. A very narrow channel — the air must squeeze through.

01
/s/vs/z/

Hiss, or buzz

Exactly the same mouth. Only the voice changes.

Your mouth

  1. Spread your lips a little, like a small smile.
  2. Bring the front of your tongue near the tooth ridge and leave a very narrow channel.
  3. Push the air through that channel.

The one difference

/s/ voice off — a strong, high hiss.
/z/ voice on — weaker friction, and your throat vibrates.

Put your hand on your throat: /zzzz/ shakes, /ssss/ does not. Now alternate /sssszzzzssss/ without moving anything.

An extra free clue

At the end of a word the vowel tells you the answer: short vowel → /s/, long vowel → /z/.

peace (short) · peas (long)

How it is written

/s/

s see, yes, usss class c + i e y city, facesc science se after C sense, falsex = /ks/ six, expect

/z/

z zoo, quizzz buzz s between vowels easy, musicse after V these, lose -s / -es dogs, washesis · was · has · his · as

Noun /s/ → verb /z/: advice–advise · use–use · excuse–excuse · house–house · close–close.

Listen

/s/

/z/

Ojo, hispanohablantes

snow, small, slow start with a clean /s/ — never /z/.

And never add an e in front: say state, not estate. Make the /s/ long and glue it to the word before: a big_state.

02
/θ/vs/ð/

Tongue on the teeth

Not /t/. Not /s/. Not /d/.

Your mouth

  1. Put the tip of your tongue lightly on the cutting edge of your upper front teeth.
  2. Do not push the tongue out. In a mirror you should see only a little of it.
  3. Keep the contact loose and blow. The tongue is flat and a lot of air comes out.

The one difference

/θ/ voice off — think, three, mouth.
/ð/ voice on — the, mother, breathe.

Same position. Alternate /θθθðððð/ and change nothing but the voice.

How it is written

/θ/ th- in content words think, three /θ/ -th at the end mouth, health /ð/ th- in function words the, they, this /ð/ th between vowels mother, leather /ð/ -the at the end breathe, soothe

Noun /θ/ → verb /ð/: breath–breathe · bath–bathe · cloth–clothe · teeth–teethe.

Listen

/θ/

/ð/

Ojo

/ð/ is soft and weak — it is never a hard /d/. In the, them, another say it quickly and quietly.

Link the to the next vowel: the apples runs together as one word.

03
/ʃ/vs/tʃ/

Blow, or stop then blow

One is a river. The other is a dam that breaks.

Your mouth

  1. Move the front of your tongue a little further back than for /s/.
  2. Round your lips slightly. Let the tongue relax.
  3. Blow: the hiss is lower and softer than /s/.

The one difference

/ʃ/ the air never stops — she, wish.
/tʃ/ stop the air completely first, then open into /ʃ/ — cheap, watch.

Both are voice off. The stop is the whole difference.

How it is written

/ʃ/

sh she, wish-tion / -cian / -ssion station, musician, permission -tial / -cial partial, specialch from French machine, Chicago su sugar, suressu pressure, issue

/tʃ/

ch church, muchtch kitchen, watch -ture nature, picture, future-tu- situation, actual ti after s question, suggestion

Listen

/ʃ/

/tʃ/

Ojo

The ending -tion is /ʃən/: sta-shun, not sta-see-on, not sta-tion.

04
/dʒ/vs/y/

Stop, or slide

jello and yellow are not the same word.

Your mouth

  1. For /dʒ/ start like /d/: stop the air completely.
  2. Open slowly and make friction — you must be able to hear it.
  3. Round the lips a little. Make the vowel before it longer.

The one difference

/dʒ/ = stop + friction — jello, judge.
/y/ = no stop, no friction. It is a fast /i/ sliding into the vowel — yellow, you.

And /dʒ/ vs /tʃ/: same mouth, /dʒ/ has the voice on.

How it is written

/dʒ/

j judge, jokeg + e i y George, giant, gym dge edge, knowledgedu gradual, educate, schedule

/y/

y you, yeari between C and V onion, million u / ue = /yu/ music, cute, human, valueew / eu few, Europe

Listen

/dʒ/

/y/

Ojo, hispanohablantes

Make real friction even in weak syllables: energy is /ˈɛnɚdʒi/ — never ener-yi.

And remember to add the /y/: music, cute, future, human, menu.

05
/ʒ/

The soft one

The rarest sound in the chapter — and the least dangerous.

Your mouth

  1. Make /ʃ/.
  2. Turn the voice on and keep it soft and continuous.
  3. Do not stop the air — that would turn it into /dʒ/.

The one difference

It only appears in the middle or at the end of words. Never at the beginning.

measure · vision · garage

How it is written

ge from French beige, garage, massagesi vision, occasion, Asian su pleasure, measure, usuallyzu azure, seizure

Families: explode → explosion → explosive · permit → permission → permissive.

Listen

/ʒ/

Relax

Saying /ʃ/ instead of /ʒ/ is a small mistake, not a serious one. Many Americans even say final garage with /dʒ/.

06
/ts/vs/dz/

Two sounds, one ending

The ending that everybody drops. advanced

Your mouth

  1. Stop the air with the tongue on the tooth ridge — a real /t/ or /d/.
  2. Open just a little and finish with /s/ or /z/.
  3. Do not cut the word short. Both sounds must be there.

The one difference

/ts/ voice off — rates, lights.
/dz/ voice on — roads, needs. Make the vowel before it longer.

/ts/ is easier. Start there, then add the voice.

The trick that fixes it

Link the ending to the next word and it becomes easy:

he needs_it → /hi nid zɪt/ · she rides_it → /ʃi raɪd zɪt/

Listen

/ts/

/dz/

Ojo

Do not turn them into /tʃ/ or /dʒ/: mats is not match, buds is not budge.

07
/f/·/v/·/b/·/w/

Lip to teeth, or lip to lip

Spanish gives you b and v for free. English does not.

Your mouth

  1. /f/ /v/ — the inside of the lower lip moves up and touches the upper teeth. In a mirror you should clearly see your two front teeth.
  2. /b/ — both lips come tightly together and stop the air completely.
  3. /w/ — a very fast /u/. Round both lips tightly; the tongue tip stays down.

The one difference

/f/ voice off · /v/ voice on.
/v/ keeps flowing · /b/ stops.
/v/ uses the teeth · /w/ never touches them.

How it is written

/f/ f, ff four, off/f/ ph photograph /f/ gh laugh, enough/v/ v very, arrive /w/ w, wh was, when/w/ u after q g s quiet, language

Verb /v/ → noun /f/: leave–leaf · save–safe · prove–proof · believe–belief.

Listen

/f/

/v/ /w/

Ojo, hispanohablantes

English /v/ is not the Spanish b. The teeth must touch the lip and the air must keep coming out.

And do not drop the /w/: woman, would, wood, question, quiet, twelve.

08
/h/

Just breath

The easiest sound to make, and the easiest to overdo.

Your mouth

  1. Open your throat as if you were simply breathing out.
  2. Do not move your tongue — it is already in the position of the vowel that follows.
  3. No scraping noise at the back. Very relaxed, then start the vowel.

The one difference

With /h/ or without it, the word changes completely: hate / ate · heart / art · hair / air.

Voice off. If you hear friction at the back of your mouth, it is too strong.

When it is silent

hour, honest, honor, heirexhaust, exhibit, ghost, rhythm wh = /h/ who, whose, wholeweak words in fast speech tell him → tellim

Listen

/h/

Ojo, hispanohablantes

English /h/ is much softer than the Spanish j of jamón. Take the friction out and just let the air go.

09
/r/vs/l/

Touch, or don't touch

One rule decides everything here.

Your mouth

  1. /r/ — point the tongue tip at the roof of the mouth but never touch it. Raise the sides against the back teeth. Round the lips a little.
  2. /l/ — the tongue tip touches the tooth ridge and the air escapes over the sides. No lip rounding.
  3. At the end of a word both are long: car, wall. Give them time.

The one difference

/l/ touches. /r/ does not. That is the whole test.

English /r/ is never the Spanish rr of perro and never the tap of pero. Nothing vibrates.

Listen

/r/

/l/

Ojo

In groups, make the /r/ or /l/ long enough or it disappears: pray / play, fright / flight, crowd / cloud.

Start moving the tongue during the /p, t, k/, not after it.

10
/m/·/n/·/ŋ/

Where the air stops

Three doors closed in three different places.

Your mouth

  1. In all three the air comes out through the nose.
  2. /m/ both lips closed · /n/ tongue tip on the tooth ridge · /ŋ/ back of the tongue on the soft palate.
  3. At the end of a word, hold them long. Never chop them off.

The one difference

Say some — son — sung very slowly and feel the closure move backwards through your mouth.

Before a voiceless consonant they get short and sharp: sing/sink · ten/tent · lamb/lamp.

The silent letters

never say the g sing, singing, singer, strong but you do say it finger, hunger, stronger, single, angry silent b comb, climb, dumb, plumber silent n autumn, column, condemn silent k / p / g knife, knee, pneumonia, sign

Listen

/m/ /n/ /ŋ/

Ojo, hispanohablantes

The ending -ing is /ɪŋ/ — the back of the tongue goes up. It is not /in/.

Say working, studying, listening and check that your tongue tip is not touching the ridge.
